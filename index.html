Operations analytics · Food distribution

Turning operational data into decisions

Shamrock Foods Company · Operations Analytics, Business Analyst
Phoenix, AZMay 31 – Jul 31, 2026

Shamrock is one of the largest privately held foodservice distributors in the U.S. On the Operations Analytics team, I led two individual projects and one team project, working in Databricks (SQL), Power BI and Excel.

Problem

Warehouse staffing in Arizona and Colorado was set by supervisor intuition, causing overtime on light days and dock congestion on peak days.

What I did

Analyzed two years of inbound data in SQL to forecast volume by day, shift and zone. Built productivity benchmarks per position, turned them into a labor formula, and designed a Power BI planning dashboard for supervisors. Validated the model against history and with supervisor feedback.

Result

Published in Shamrock’s Power BI environment as a production tool. Supervisors started using it right away, and leadership said it exceeded expectations.

Problem

Weekly driver schedules (“bids”) were built by hand. Fewer, better bids mean lower labor cost and better driver retention in a tight labor market.

What I did

Framed bid building as a constrained optimization problem with DOT rest rules, weekly hour limits, license classes and days off. Cross-checked planned against GPS-tracked route times to set safe buffers, then built a greedy heuristic with a local search improvement step.

Result

Delivered a documented, repeatable framework and data pipeline for the transportation planning team. The team said the analysis exceeded expectations.

Problem

Were Shamrock’s fees for product recalls and withdrawals covering the true cost, and being billed and collected consistently?

What I did

In a team project, ran 30+ interviews across seven departments to map every recall activity. Built a fully loaded labor cost model for each fee and audited recent recall events to measure under-recovery and collection gaps.

Result

Presented to about 100 leaders, including the company president. Recommended new fee rates, an annual review cycle and a one-page fee scorecard, and leadership committed to implementing them.
